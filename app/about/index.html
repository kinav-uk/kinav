Why I built Kinav

Kinav was built by a family carer whose father has dementia.

When my father was diagnosed with dementia, I didn't know where to start.

I was working full time. Overnight I was also learning a new language: needs assessments, Attendance Allowance, Lasting Power of Attorney, CQC ratings, care home fees. Every answer seemed to lead to three more questions, and each one was on a different website, behind a phone queue, or in a leaflet I couldn't find when I needed it.

The hardest part wasn't the forms. It was the feeling that I was always one step behind, and that one wrong decision could cost my father his home, his savings or his dignity.

So I started writing things down: the questions to ask, the letters that worked, which care homes near us were actually rated well, what the council had to do and what it didn't.

Kinav is those notes, made into something anyone can use.

What I promised myself when I built it

  • Free where it matters. Finding care, working out costs, checking benefits and writing to the council will always be free.
  • Only good care. We only list care rated Good or Outstanding by CQC, and care homes can't pay to be listed.
  • Nothing about your family is stored. No sign-up, no selling your details.
  • Plain English. Written for a tired person at 11pm, not for a professional.

Kinav doesn't give medical or legal advice. It points you to the people who can, like your GP, Citizens Advice, Carers UK and Age UK. What it does is help you feel less alone, and a little more in control.

If you're caring for someone right now: you're doing better than you think.
