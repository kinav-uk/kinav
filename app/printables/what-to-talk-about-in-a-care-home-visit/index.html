What to talk about when visiting someone in a care home

Easy ideas and 15 conversation starters for visiting a parent or grandparent in a care home, so visits feel less awkward and more like time together.

8 ideas that help

  1. Bring something to look at
    Photos, a magazine, post from home, a newspaper or something from the garden. Things to look at make it easy to start talking.
  2. Talk about now, too
    What the family are up to, what's on TV, the weather, the football. Everyday news matters.
  3. Ask open questions
    "Tell me about…" works better than questions with a yes or no answer.
  4. Do something together
    A crossword, a card game, a jigsaw, nails or hair, a walk round the garden. Talking is easier side by side.
  5. Play music
    Their favourite songs on your phone. Sing along if they want to.
  6. Silence is fine
    Sitting together quietly, holding hands or watching the birds still counts as a good visit.
  7. Keep it short and regular
    Shorter, more frequent visits often go better than long ones.
  8. Say when you'll be back
    Write it on their calendar, so they can look forward to it.

15 conversation starters

  1. What have you been up to this week?
  2. Who's been in to see you?
  3. What was lunch like today?
  4. What's the best thing about living here?
  5. Is there anything you'd like me to bring next time?
  6. What did you think of the weather this week?
  7. Which of the staff makes you laugh?
  8. What's on the telly that you're enjoying?
  9. Shall we look at some photos?
  10. What were you doing at my age?
  11. Can you tell me again how you met Grandad (or Grandma)?
  12. What was your favourite holiday?
  13. What should I cook this weekend? How did you make it?
  14. Which song shall we put on?
  15. What would you like to do next time I come?
