Free printable "No trick or treaters" door sign

For an older parent, relative or neighbour who would rather not have knocks at the door after dark on Halloween. Print it, then tape it to the front door or a window.

🎃
No trick or treaters, pleaseThank you, and have a lovely Halloween

A few other things that help

  • Remind them they don't have to answer the door after dark, to anyone they aren't expecting.
  • Leave the porch light off if they don't want callers. Many families take that as a sign to skip the house.
  • Give them a quick call in the evening, or ask a neighbour to look in.
  • If a stranger calls about work on the house or money, they can say no and close the door. Real callers won't mind.
