Emergency information sheet for an older parent: free printable

One page with what paramedics, carers or a neighbour need to know in a hurry. Type in the details, print it, and keep it on the fridge. What you type stays on this page: it isn't saved or sent anywhere.

In an emergency

Call 999

Free from kinav.co.uk · Check and update it every few months.

Tips

  • Keep it on the fridge door, where paramedics are used to looking, and give a copy to whoever has a spare key.
  • Leave a box blank to fill in by hand after printing.
  • For medicines, write where the list is kept rather than copying it out, so there's only one list to keep up to date. The pharmacy can print one.
  • Some areas give out free "Message in a Bottle" kits from the Lions Clubs, with stickers that tell paramedics to look in the fridge.
