Tips for a happy quiz

  • Read each question twice, slowly, and give both answers.
  • Play in teams, so nobody is put on the spot.
  • Let the questions lead to stories: "Did you have a sixpence in your pudding?"
  • Stop while it's still fun. Ten questions is plenty for some people.
