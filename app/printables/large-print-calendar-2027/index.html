Big, clear dates with room to write appointments, visits and bin days. Pick a month, then print it on A4. England and Wales bank holidays and the clock changes are marked.

Tips

  • Print next month as well, and keep it behind this one on the wall or fridge.
  • Cross off each day at bedtime, so the next morning it's easy to see what day it is.
  • Use one colour for visits from family, and another for appointments.
