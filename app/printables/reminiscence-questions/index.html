10 reminiscence questions for older people

Gentle questions to start conversations about a long life: growing up, work, love, music, holidays and more. For visits, care home groups or a cup of tea at home. Free to print.

How to use them

  • Pick one question, not a list. Let the answer lead where it wants to go.
  • Share a memory of your own, so it feels like a chat, not a quiz.
  • Photos, music and old objects help memories come.
  • If a question doesn't feel right, move on. "Pass" is always fine.
  • Write down or record the best stories for the family.

10 questions to start with

  1. Where did you grow up, and what was the house like?
  2. What games did you play outside as a child?
  3. Who was your favourite teacher?
  4. What was your first job, and what did you spend your first wage on?
  5. How did you meet someone special?
  6. What was the first record you bought?
  7. Where did you go on holiday as a child?
  8. What was Christmas like when you were young?
  9. What's the biggest change you've seen in your lifetime?
  10. What's the best advice you've ever been given?
