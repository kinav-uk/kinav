Before they leave hospital: questions for the ward

A one-page sheet to print and take to the ward, or to have by the phone. Write the answers as you go, so nothing is missed and everyone in the family knows the plan.

Before they leave hospital

Name:Ward:Hospital:Date:

The plan

When is the planned discharge date and time?
Can we have the written discharge plan, and a copy for the family?
Who is our named contact for the discharge, and their number?

Support at home

What care and support will be in place at home, and from which day?
Is reablement being arranged? It's free, usually for up to 6 weeks.
Who is arranging the care, and how do we contact them?
Has a care needs assessment been done or asked for?

Equipment and the home

What equipment is needed, and when will it be delivered?
Does someone need to check the home before they come back, such as an occupational therapist?

Medicines

What medicines are they going home with, and who will explain any changes?

Getting home

Who is arranging transport, and what time?
Will the GP get a discharge letter, and can we have a copy?
Has the NHS continuing healthcare checklist been considered?
Who do we call if something agreed doesn't happen?
Please don't assume family can provide the care.
