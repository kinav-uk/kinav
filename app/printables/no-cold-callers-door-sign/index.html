Free printable "No cold callers" door sign

Doorstep sellers and scammers often target older people. A clear sign on the door helps them say no. Print it, then tape it to the door or a window by the door.

No cold callersWe don't buy or sign up to anything at the door.
Please respect this notice and leave.

Help them stay safe at the door

  • They never have to open the door to someone they aren't expecting. A door chain or a video doorbell helps them check first.
  • Genuine callers from gas, electricity and water companies carry ID and won't mind waiting while they phone the company on a number from a bill, not one the caller gives them.
  • No genuine bank, police officer or council will ask for their PIN, or send someone to collect cash or their bank card.
  • Be wary of anyone who knocks offering roof, driveway or garden work, especially if they want cash or to start today.
  • If it happens, report it to Citizens Advice on 0808 223 1133, who pass it to Trading Standards. If someone won't leave or they feel unsafe, call 999.
