Letter to challenge a council care decision: free template

If you think an assessment, a care plan or a funding decision is wrong.

Fill in the gaps on your phone and Kinav writes the letter for you, ready to email, copy or print. Nothing you type leaves your phone.

Write this letter

The letter

[your name]
[your phone or email]
[date]

Adult Social Care
[council]

Dear Adult Social Care team,

Request to review a decision: [their name]

I'm writing about a decision the council made on [date of the decision] about [their name], my [daughter or son].

The council decided [what was decided].

We disagree because [why you disagree].

Please:
- send me the decision and the reasons for it in writing, if you haven't already
- send me a copy of the assessment and any care and support plan
- review the decision, taking these points into account

[their name] knows I'm writing and agrees.

If we can't sort this out, I'll make a formal complaint.

Yours sincerely,

[your name]

Lines you can switch on or off in the app

  • They know I'm writing for them

Good to know

  • Email or post it, and keep a copy with the date you sent it.
  • If you haven't heard back in a couple of weeks, phone and ask who is dealing with it.
  • Free help: Carers UK on 0808 808 7777 and Age UK on 0800 678 1602. Citizens Advice: 0800 144 8848.

Sources: GOV.UK: apply for a needs assessment · Citizens Advice. Last reviewed 24 October 2026.
