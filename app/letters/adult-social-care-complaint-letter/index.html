Adult social care complaint letter: free template

If things have gone wrong with the council's adult social care and asking hasn't fixed it.

Fill in the gaps on your phone and Kinav writes the letter for you, ready to email, copy or print. Nothing you type leaves your phone.

Write this letter

The letter

[your name]
[your phone or email]
[date]

Complaints team, Adult Social Care
[council]

Dear complaints team,

Formal complaint: [their name]

I'm making a formal complaint about the council's adult social care, about the care and support for [their name] (I'm their [daughter or son]).

What happened:
[what happened]

What I'd like to happen:
[what you'd like to happen]

[their name] knows I'm writing and agrees.

Please acknowledge this complaint within 3 working days, tell me who is dealing with it, and let me know when to expect a full response.

If I'm still unhappy after your response, I understand I can ask the Local Government and Social Care Ombudsman to look into it.

Yours sincerely,

[your name]

Lines you can switch on or off in the app

  • They know I'm writing for them

Good to know

  • Email or post it, and keep a copy with the date you sent it.
  • If you haven't heard back in a couple of weeks, phone and ask who is dealing with it.
  • Free help: Carers UK on 0808 808 7777 and Age UK on 0800 678 1602. Citizens Advice: 0800 144 8848.

Sources: Local Government and Social Care Ombudsman · Citizens Advice. Last reviewed 24 October 2026.
