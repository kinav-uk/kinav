Subject access request for care records: free template

A subject access request for their care plans, daily notes and visit records. It's free, and the reply is due within one month.

Fill in the gaps on your phone and Kinav writes the letter for you, ready to email, copy or print. Nothing you type leaves your phone.

Write this letter

The letter

[your name]
[your phone or email]
[date]

Data Protection Officer
[name of the care home, agency or service]

Dear Data Protection Officer,

Subject access request: [their name]

I'm making a subject access request under the UK General Data Protection Regulation for the personal data [name of the care home, agency or service] holds about [their name], of [their address].

I'm [their name]'s attorney under a Lasting Power of Attorney, and a copy is attached.

Please send everything from [records from (date, optional)] onwards, including:
- care and support plans, and risk assessments
- daily care notes and visit records
- electronic call monitoring (check-in and check-out) times
- incident, accident and medicines records
- emails, messages and notes about [their name]

I understand you must reply within one month, and that there's normally no fee.

Please send the information by email if you can.

Yours sincerely,

[your name]

Lines you can switch on or off in the app

  • They've agreed I can ask for their records (signed consent attached)
  • I'm their attorney under a Lasting Power of Attorney (copy attached)

Good to know

  • Email or post it, and keep a copy with the date you sent it.
  • If you haven't heard back in a couple of weeks, phone and ask who is dealing with it.
  • Free help: Carers UK on 0808 808 7777 and Age UK on 0800 678 1602. Citizens Advice: 0800 144 8848.

Sources: ICO: your right of access. Last reviewed 24 October 2026.
