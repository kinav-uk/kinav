Sudden confusion (delirium)

Confusion that starts over hours or days needs urgent medical help. It is often treatable.

Follow this plan and tick off each step in the free Kinav app.

Start the plan

Right now

  • Get medical help the same day
    Call the GP urgently or NHS 111. If you think it's an emergency, call 999.
  • Tell the doctor what's normal for them
    Say how they were a few days ago, so the doctor can see what has changed.
  • Tell them about any recent changes
    New medicines, a recent fall, or anything else that's different.

While they recover

  • Ask the staff how you can help
    Ask the GP, nurse or ward what you can do while they recover.
  • Bring their glasses and hearing aids
    And familiar things from home, if they're in hospital.
  • Stay with them if you can
    A familiar face is calming. In hospital, ask about open visiting for carers.
  • Share a This is me form in hospital
    From Alzheimer's Society: tells staff about their routine, likes and how to calm them.

Afterwards

  • Ask what to expect
    Ask the doctor how recovery is likely to go.
  • Ask the GP for a follow-up
    Ask whether any further checks are needed once they've recovered.
  • Ask what to do if it happens again
    Ask the GP, and write the plan down.
