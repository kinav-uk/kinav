Diabetes in older age

Help with diabetes as needs change in later life.

Follow this plan and tick off each step in the free Kinav app.

Start the plan

Week 1: understand the plan

  • Ask for the diabetes care plan
    What medicines, what targets, and what to do if they feel unwell.
  • Save the Diabetes UK helpline
    0345 123 2399. Advice from trained counsellors.
  • Ask about hypos
    Ask the diabetes nurse what a hypo looks like for them and what you should do.
  • Ask about sick day rules
    Some diabetes medicines need changing when someone is ill. Ask the GP or nurse.

Weeks 2 to 3: regular checks

  • Book the yearly diabetes review
    Blood tests, blood pressure, kidney check and a medicines review.
  • Ask about foot checks
    Ask the nurse what to look out for, and make sure they have their yearly foot check.
  • Go to diabetic eye screening
    Free every year or two. It finds damage before sight is affected.
  • Book flu, COVID and pneumonia vaccines
    Free on the NHS for older people. Ask the GP or pharmacy.

Weeks 3 to 4: care and you

  • Ask if their care plan still fits
    Needs change with age. Ask at the yearly review whether anything should change.
  • Claim Attendance Allowance or PIP
    Attendance Allowance if over State Pension age, Personal Independence Payment if under. Use the Benefits calculator.
  • Ask the council for a needs assessment and a carer's assessment
    Both are free, whatever their savings.
  • Check Carer's Allowance and Carer's Credit
    Carer's Credit from 20 hours a week, Carer's Allowance from 35.
