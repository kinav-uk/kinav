Living with heart failure

Your first 30 days after a heart failure diagnosis, one step at a time.

Follow this plan and tick off each step in the free Kinav app.

Start the plan

Week 1: daily checks

  • Ask for a heart failure nurse
    Ask the GP or hospital team for a referral. They are your main contact between appointments.
  • Save the British Heart Foundation helpline
    0808 802 1234. Advice from cardiac nurses.
  • Ask the nurse what checks to do at home
    Ask what to record, and what changes to tell them about.
  • Ask when to call for help
    Ask the GP or nurse when to call them, NHS 111 or 999, and keep the numbers in one place.

Weeks 2 to 3: medicines and activity

  • Keep a list of their medicines
    Take it to every appointment. Ask the pharmacist or GP any questions about it.
  • Ask about cardiac rehabilitation
    Supervised exercise and advice that helps people with heart failure feel better.
  • Ask about diet and drinks
    Ask the heart failure nurse whether there is anything they should change.
  • Book flu, COVID and pneumonia vaccines
    Free on the NHS for older people. Ask the GP or pharmacy.
  • Claim Attendance Allowance or PIP
    Attendance Allowance if over State Pension age, Personal Independence Payment if under. Use the Benefits calculator.

Weeks 3 to 4: care and you

  • Ask the council for a needs assessment and a carer's assessment
    Both are free, whatever their savings.
  • Make both Lasting Powers of Attorney
    Property and financial affairs, and health and welfare, while they can make the decision.
  • Check Carer's Allowance and Carer's Credit
    Carer's Credit from 20 hours a week, Carer's Allowance from 35.
  • Tell your employer
    One week of unpaid carer's leave a year, and the right to ask for flexible working, both from day one.

General information only, not health or medical advice. For health questions, speak to the GP, a pharmacist or NHS 111. In an emergency, call 999.
