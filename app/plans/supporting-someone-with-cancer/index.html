Supporting someone with cancer

Your first 30 days after a cancer diagnosis, one step at a time.

Follow this plan and tick off each step in the free Kinav app.

Start the plan

Week 1: know the team

  • Get the name of their key worker
    Usually a clinical nurse specialist. Your main contact for questions.
  • Save the Macmillan Support Line
    0808 808 00 00. Help with treatment, money, work and feelings.
  • Ask for the treatment plan in writing
    What treatment, where, how often, and what side effects to watch for.
  • Know who to call if they become unwell during treatment
    Many hospitals have a 24-hour chemotherapy or oncology hotline. Save the number.

Weeks 2 to 3: money and travel

  • Claim Attendance Allowance or PIP
    Attendance Allowance if over State Pension age, Personal Independence Payment if under. Use the Benefits calculator.
  • Ask about help with travel to hospital
    The Healthcare Travel Costs Scheme refunds fares for people on low incomes. Hospitals may have volunteer drivers.
  • Ask Macmillan about grants and benefits advice
    Macmillan offers one-off grants and free benefits advice.
  • Apply for a Blue Badge if walking is hard
    Hospital parking can also be free or reduced. Ask the hospital.

Weeks 3 to 4: care and you

  • Ask the council for a needs assessment and a carer's assessment
    Both are free, whatever their savings.
  • Make both Lasting Powers of Attorney
    Property and financial affairs, and health and welfare, while they can make the decision.
  • Check Carer's Allowance and Carer's Credit
    Carer's Credit from 20 hours a week, Carer's Allowance from 35.
  • Tell your employer
    One week of unpaid carer's leave a year, and the right to ask for flexible working, both from day one.
