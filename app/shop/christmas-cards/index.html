Let's Talk Christmas pack

Christmas reminiscence cards, a quiz and word search to share Christmas memories.

Conversation cards, a quiz and word searches. A complete Christmas activity pack for families: share stories, laughter and memories with an older parent or grandparent this Christmas. Lovely for visits, Christmas Day, and as a thoughtful gift.

A look inside

What's inside

  • 144 conversation cards: 24 Christmas memories (stockings, carols, pantomimes, the sixpence in the pudding), 96 cards in 12 themes, and 24 'Do you remember…?' cards
  • Large, easy-to-read text, with a gentle follow-up question on every card
  • Pretty hand-drawn Christmas designs, and red and green card backs for double-sided printing
  • A large-print Christmas quiz: 20 questions, with answers
  • 2 large-print Christmas word searches, with answers
  • Memory tree: bauble cut-outs to write a Christmas memory on and hang up
  • Christmas table place cards with a conversation question for each guest
  • A 12 Days of Christmas activity plan
  • A printable 'A gift of stories' certificate, to give the pack as a present
  • Tips for a good conversation, and pages to keep the stories

How it works

  • Instant download: one A4 PDF, 37 pages
  • Print the cards, baubles and place cards on A4 card (about 250gsm), and the quiz and word searches on paper
  • Print as many copies as you need for your family
