Retro Christmas games: care home edition

Four decades of Christmas quizzes, with an easy mode, licensed for a care home or day centre.

Four decades of quizzes, bingo and carols, licensed for your home. A ready-made retro Christmas games afternoon for care homes, day centres and activity coordinators. Quizzes from four decades, so residents, staff and visitors can all play, with an easy mode for residents who need it, and a licence to print as often as you like for your home.

A look inside

What's inside

  • Four Christmas quizzes, one for each decade: the 1950s, 1960s, 1970s and 1980s, with 24 multiple-choice questions each (sixpences and Matchbox cars, the Beatles and Thunderbirds, Space Hoppers and Slade, Band Aid and Rubik's Cubes)
  • Easy mode: 12 questions from each decade with just two answers, in extra-large print, for anyone with tired eyes or memory problems
  • 'Do you remember…?' cards: 24 conversation cards, 6 for each decade, to turn quiz answers into stories
  • Answers for every quiz
  • Christmas bingo: 16 different boards, 30 calling cards and a caller's checklist
  • Finish the carol: 24 cards with traditional carols, with the answer on each card
  • Christmas charades: 24 cards to act out, all fine to do sitting down
  • Retro card backs for double-sided printing
  • Score sheet for teams, with a column for every round
  • A games afternoon plan for about two hours, with tips for running it
  • Care home licence page

How it works

  • Instant download: one A4 PDF, 51 pages
  • Print the quizzes, bingo boards and score sheets on paper, and the cards on A4 card (about 250gsm)
