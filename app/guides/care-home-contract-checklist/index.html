Before you sign a care home contract

Fees, increases, top-ups and notice.

Tick off each step as you go in the free Kinav app.

Open this checklist

Step by step

  1. Get the contract in advance
    Ask for it before moving day, and take time to read it. Check who is signing and in what role: only sign as their attorney if you have a Lasting Power of Attorney, and don't agree to pay fees personally unless you mean to.
  2. Check the fees
    What is the weekly fee, and what costs extra, such as hairdressing, trips or toiletries?
  3. Check how fees go up
    How often do fees rise, by how much, and how much notice do they give?
  4. Ask about top-up fees
    If the council starts paying later, will someone need to pay a top-up? Who agrees to it?
  5. Check deposits and payment in advance
    How much, and when is it returned?
  6. Check notice terms
    How much notice is needed to leave, and how many days of fees are charged after a death?
  7. Check when a resident can be asked to leave
    Ask what the reasons are and what notice they give.
  8. Know how to complain
    Ask for the complaints process. If it isn't resolved, you can go to the Local Government and Social Care Ombudsman.
  9. Get free help if anything is unclear
    Citizens Advice can explain your rights on fees, notice and unfair terms. Call the Citizens Advice consumer helpline on 0808 223 1133, or the Adviceline (England) on 0800 144 8848.
