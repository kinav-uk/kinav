Leaving hospital

Make sure the right help is in place at home.

Tick off each step as you go in the free Kinav app.

Open this checklist

Step by step

  1. Ask who is planning the discharge
    Ask the ward for the name of the person coordinating it, and for the discharge plan.
  2. Be clear about what you can do
    If you can't provide care, say so. Don't let the plan assume family will cover.
  3. Ask about free short-term care
    Ask about reablement or intermediate care to help them recover at home.
  4. Ask about NHS Continuing Healthcare
    If health needs are complex, ask for the Continuing Healthcare checklist before discharge.
  5. Get equipment in place
    Make sure equipment such as a commode or raised toilet seat is delivered before they come home.
  6. Sort out medicines
    Ask for a list of medicines, who will collect them, and whether the pharmacy can provide blister packs.
  7. Check the follow-up
    Make sure the GP has the discharge letter, and ask whether a district nurse will visit.

Sources: NHS: social care and support guide · Carers UK: help and advice. Last reviewed 24 October 2026.
