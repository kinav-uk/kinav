Home safety and grants

Free OT assessment, free equipment and grants.

Tick off each step as you go in the free Kinav app.

Open this checklist

Step by step

  1. Ask for a free OT home assessment
    Call the council's adult social care team and ask for an occupational therapist to assess the home. Ask how long the wait is, and tell them about any falls or near misses, as this can speed things up.
  2. Ask what equipment is free
    In England, councils should provide equipment and small adaptations costing under £1,000 free of charge.
  3. Ask about a Disabled Facilities Grant
    Up to £30,000 in England for bigger changes such as a stairlift or level-access shower. It is means-tested, and usually needs an OT to recommend the work first. Don't start the work before it's approved, or it may not be paid. Tenants can apply too, with the landlord's permission.
  4. Book a free fire safety visit
    Your local fire and rescue service checks smoke alarms and fire risks.
  5. Consider a fall alarm
    Ask the council about telecare, such as a pendant alarm or fall detector. See the Fall alarms and home cameras kit.
  6. Use trusted traders
    Ask the council about its trusted trader scheme, and get 2 or 3 written quotes.

Sources: GOV.UK: Disabled Facilities Grants · NHS: falls. Last reviewed 24 October 2026.
