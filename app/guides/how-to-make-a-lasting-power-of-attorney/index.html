Making a Lasting Power of Attorney

Lets someone they trust make decisions if they can't. Do it while they still can. England and Wales.

Tick off each step as you go in the free Kinav app.

Open this checklist

Step by step

  1. Know the two types
    Property and financial affairs covers the bank, bills and the house. Health and welfare covers care, treatment and where they live. Many families make both.
  2. Do it while they can
    They must understand what it means when they sign. If you're not sure they still can, see the No Lasting Power of Attorney kit.
  3. Choose the attorneys
    One or more people they trust, aged 18 or over. Decide whether attorneys must make every decision together (jointly) or can also act alone (jointly and severally).
  4. Name a replacement
    A replacement attorney steps in if an attorney can no longer act.
  5. Make it online
    Use GOV.UK (search "make a lasting power of attorney"). You can fill it in with them, but the choices must be theirs. Paper forms are available too.
  6. Choose a certificate provider
    Someone who has known them for at least 2 years, or a professional such as their GP or a solicitor, confirms they understand it and aren't under pressure. It can't be a family member or an attorney.
  7. Sign in the right order
    They sign first, then the certificate provider, then the attorneys. Signatures need a witness, and an attorney can't witness their signature.
  8. Register it and pay
    £92 for each LPA, so £184 for both (2026). There's a reduction if their income is under £12,000, and an exemption on some benefits.
  9. Allow time
    Registration takes 8 to 10 weeks if there are no mistakes. A mistake can mean starting again, so check every page before sending.
  10. Once it's registered
    Use the online service (search "use a lasting power of attorney") to share it with banks, the GP and care providers. Keep the paper copy safe.
  11. Get free help
    Office of the Public Guardian 0300 456 0300. Age UK 0800 678 1602.

Sources: GOV.UK: make a lasting power of attorney · GOV.UK: register a lasting power of attorney. Last reviewed 24 October 2026.
