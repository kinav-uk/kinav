Arranging live-in care

Find, check and plan cover for a live-in carer.

Tick off each step as you go in the free Kinav app.

Open this checklist

Step by step

  1. Choose the type of service
    A managed service employs the carer and must be registered with CQC. An introductory agency only introduces carers, so you take on more responsibility.
  2. Check the space
    A live-in carer needs their own bedroom.
  3. Compare the cost
    Ask for the weekly fee and put it into the Care costs tool to see how long savings last.
  4. Meet the carer before they start
    Ask the agency how they match carers, and what happens if it isn't working.
  5. Plan breaks and holidays
    Ask who covers the carer's daily breaks, days off and holidays.
  6. Tell the home insurer
    Let them know someone else will be living in the home.
  7. Review after the first month
    Check the care plan still fits.

Sources: Care Quality Commission (CQC) · NHS: social care and support guide. Last reviewed 24 October 2026.
