Choosing a nursing home

Shortlist, visit and ask the right questions.

Tick off each step as you go in the free Kinav app.

Open this checklist

Step by step

  1. Ask the council for a free needs assessment
    It confirms what level of care is needed. Anyone can ask, whatever their savings.
  2. Work out how it will be paid for
    Use the Care costs tool. If savings are above £23,250 they will usually pay their own fees at first. Ask the council for a financial assessment either way.
  3. Shortlist 3 to 5 homes
    Use Find care, filter to nursing homes and dementia care if needed, and read each home's latest CQC report.
  4. Phone each home
    Say: "I'm looking for a nursing place for my mum. Do you have a vacancy, what is the weekly fee, and what does it include?" Ask whether they keep residents if their savings run out and the council starts paying.
  5. Visit at least twice
    Go once at a mealtime. Look for staff talking with residents, call bells answered quickly, residents doing things, and a clean, fresh home.
  6. Ask the manager
    How many nurses are on each shift? How much do you use agency staff? How do you support someone who becomes distressed? How often does a GP visit? Can residents stay here to the end of their life?
  7. Ask about NHS help with nursing costs
    The NHS may pay a contribution towards nursing care. Ask about an NHS Continuing Healthcare check too.
  8. Read the contract before signing
    Use the Before you sign kit.
  9. Plan the move
    Bring familiar things and photos, label clothes, and tell the GP, pharmacy, bank and pension providers about the new address.

Sources: Care Quality Commission (CQC) · Age UK: care and support. Last reviewed 24 October 2026.
