Preparing for a care needs assessment

The council's free assessment: how to ask, how to prepare and what happens next. England.

Tick off each step as you go in the free Kinav app.

Open this checklist

Step by step

  1. Ask the council
    Contact adult social care at their council. Anyone who seems to need care has the right to an assessment, free, whatever their savings. Kinav can write the letter for you.
  2. Agree how it will happen
    It can be at home, by phone or by video. They can have a family member or friend with them. If they'd struggle to take part and have no one to help, the council must offer an independent advocate.
  3. Keep a diary for a week
    Note what they need help with, day and night: washing, dressing, the toilet, meals, medicines, moving around, going out and staying safe. Include the bad days, not just the good ones.
  4. Write down what family do now
    List the help you and others give, and say clearly what you can't keep doing. The plan shouldn't assume family will cover everything.
  5. Have the details to hand
    A list of their medicines, GP and hospital contacts, recent letters from health professionals, and any equipment they use.
  6. On the day
    Many older people play things down. Let them speak first, then gently add what you've seen, using examples from the diary.
  7. Ask what happens next
    Ask whether they meet the national eligibility criteria, when you'll hear in writing, and for a copy of the assessment.
  8. If they qualify
    The council writes a care and support plan with you, and a financial assessment works out what they pay. With savings over £23,250 they usually pay the full cost at first.
  9. If they don't qualify
    The council must tell you why in writing and give information about other help, such as day centres or local charities. If you disagree, ask for a review or make a complaint.
  10. Ask for a carer's assessment too
    If you help them, you have the right to your own assessment, free. Kinav can write that letter too.
  11. Get free help
    Age UK 0800 678 1602. Carers UK 0808 808 7777. Citizens Advice Adviceline (England) 0800 144 8848.

Sources: GOV.UK: apply for a needs assessment · Age UK: care and support. Last reviewed 24 October 2026.
