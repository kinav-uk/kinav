Setting up care at home

Compare agencies and agree a care plan.

Tick off each step as you go in the free Kinav app.

Open this checklist

Step by step

  1. Ask the council for a free needs assessment
    Ask about reablement too: free short-term support at home, usually for up to 6 weeks.
  2. Ask about direct payments
    If the council helps pay, you can ask for the money as a direct payment and choose the agency or carer yourself, instead of the council arranging it.
  3. List what help is needed
    Write down the times of day and tasks, for example getting up and washed, lunch and medication, help to bed.
  4. Ask about day centres and respite
    Ask the social worker or council about day centres or day services, often with transport and a meal, and short respite stays so you can have a break. Many day centres only take people referred by a social worker. The council may help pay after a financial assessment.
  5. Find 3 agencies
    Use Find care with "Care at home", and read each agency's latest CQC report.
  6. Call each agency
    Ask: What is your hourly rate, and is it higher at weekends? What is the shortest visit? Will Mum see the same carers? Are your carers trained in dementia care? What happens if a carer is late or can't come? How much notice do I give to stop?
  7. Get it in writing
    Ask for the rates, terms and notice period by email before you agree.
  8. Agree a written care plan
    The agency should visit first and write down what carers will do at each visit.
  9. Be there for the first visits
    Leave a notebook at home for carers and family to write in. A police-approved key safe lets carers get in if they can't answer the door.
  10. Review after a month
    Ask the council for a review if needs change.

Sources: GOV.UK: apply for a needs assessment · Care Quality Commission (CQC). Last reviewed 24 October 2026.
