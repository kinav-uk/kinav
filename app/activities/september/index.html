Care home activity ideas for September

Back to school, harvest and the Battle of Britain. Easy ideas for care homes, day centres and families with older relatives.

Dates to plan around in September 2027

  • 15 September: Battle of Britain Day

8 activity ideas

  1. Harvest festival
    Collect tins for a food bank and sing harvest hymns.
  2. Back to school memories
    Uniforms, satchels and the walk to school.
  3. Blackberry picking
    Pick or buy blackberries and talk about crumbles and jam.
  4. Battle of Britain memories
    Share wartime stories and look at pictures of the planes.
  5. Apple tasting
    Try different apples and vote for a favourite.
  6. Autumn leaf art
    Collect leaves and make pictures or prints.
  7. Handwriting afternoon
    Copybooks, fountain pens and best handwriting.
  8. Radio favourites
    Old radio shows and the songs people listened to.

Check dietary needs before anything is eaten, and adapt each idea so everyone can join in.
