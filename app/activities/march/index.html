Care home activity ideas for March

Mother's Day, St David's Day, St Patrick's Day and Easter. Easy ideas for care homes, day centres and families with older relatives.

Dates to plan around in March 2027

  • 1 March: St David's Day
  • 7 March: Mother's Day
  • 17 March: St Patrick's Day
  • 28 March: Easter Sunday (clocks go forward 1 hour)

8 activity ideas

  1. Mother's Day memories
    Talk about mums and grandmas: their sayings, recipes and the things they made.
  2. Daffodil crafts
    Paper or egg-box daffodils for St David's Day.
  3. A St Patrick's Day afternoon
    Irish songs, a green quiz and soda bread.
  4. Easter bonnets
    Decorate paper or straw hats and hold a gentle parade.
  5. Egg decorating
    Hard-boiled or paper eggs, with pens, paint and stickers.
  6. Spring planting
    Sow cress or bulbs in pots for the windowsill.
  7. Easter memories
    Hot cross buns, egg rolling and Easter bonnets in years gone by.
  8. An Easter egg hunt
    A simple hunt indoors or in the garden, with prizes for everyone.

Check dietary needs before anything is eaten, and adapt each idea so everyone can join in.
