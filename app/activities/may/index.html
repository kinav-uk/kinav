Care home activity ideas for May

Bank holidays, maypoles and getting out into the garden. Easy ideas for care homes, day centres and families with older relatives.

Dates to plan around in May 2027

  • 3 May: Early May bank holiday
  • 31 May: Spring bank holiday

8 activity ideas

  1. A May Day ribbon dance
    A small maypole or ribbons on sticks, with music.
  2. Garden afternoon
    Sit out, pot up plants or deadhead flowers together.
  3. Seaside day trip memories
    Bank holiday trips, the coach, the beach and fish and chips.
  4. Flower crowns
    Paper or real flowers threaded into crowns.
  5. Cream tea
    Scones, jam and cream. Jam first or cream first?
  6. Old adverts quiz
    Slogans and jingles from old TV and radio adverts.
  7. Nature table
    Collect leaves, flowers and stones and make a display.
  8. Bank holiday sing-along
    Music hall and wartime songs everyone knows.

Check dietary needs before anything is eaten, and adapt each idea so everyone can join in.
