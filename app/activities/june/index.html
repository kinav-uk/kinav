Care home activity ideas for June

Summer, Father's Day and Armed Forces Day. Easy ideas for care homes, day centres and families with older relatives.

Dates to plan around in June 2027

  • 20 June: Father's Day
  • 21 June: Longest day of the year
  • 26 June: Armed Forces Day

8 activity ideas

  1. Father's Day memories
    Talk about dads and grandads: their jobs, sayings and hobbies.
  2. A garden party
    Bunting, squash, sandwiches and music outside.
  3. Strawberries and cream
    Taste strawberries and talk about tennis and summer sport.
  4. Armed Forces Day
    Share service memories and family stories, and make bunting.
  5. Summer holidays then
    Holiday camps, caravans and seaside boarding houses.
  6. Garden games
    Skittles, hoopla and bean bags, played seated or standing.
  7. Pressed flowers
    Press flowers from the garden and make cards.
  8. Picnic indoors or out
    Pack a picnic, spread a blanket and enjoy it together.

Check dietary needs before anything is eaten, and adapt each idea so everyone can join in.
