Care home activity ideas for July

Summer fun, the seaside and sports days. Easy ideas for care homes, day centres and families with older relatives.

8 activity ideas

  1. Seaside afternoon
    Sand in a tray, shells, ice lollies and seaside songs.
  2. Sports day games
    Egg and spoon, beanbag throwing and a gentle obstacle course.
  3. Ice cream tasting
    Try three flavours and talk about ice cream vans and wafers.
  4. School days memories
    School milk, slates, teachers and the last day of term.
  5. Summer songs
    Songs about summer and holidays, with shakers.
  6. Outdoor painting
    Paint flowers or the view from the garden.
  7. Holiday postcards
    Write postcards to family, or look at old ones.
  8. Fruit kebabs
    Make and share fruit kebabs, after checking dietary needs.

Check dietary needs before anything is eaten, and adapt each idea so everyone can join in.
