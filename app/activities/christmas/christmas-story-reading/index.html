A Christmas story read aloud

Read 'Twas the Night Before Christmas or part of A Christmas Carol, slowly, with feeling.

For care homes, day centres and families with older relatives. Takes about 30 to 45 minutes.

You'll need

  • A copy of the story: both are free online
  • A cosy, quiet room

How to run it

  1. Choose a short story or one chapter.
  2. Read slowly and clearly, with pauses.
  3. Stop to ask: "Who remembers this one?"
  4. Let anyone who'd like to read a few lines.

Make it easier

  • Short is better than long.
  • Pictures from the book help people follow.
