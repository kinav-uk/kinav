Day 16 of 24

Christmas around the world

A tour of Christmas traditions from other countries, with a chat about the ones people know.

For care homes, day centres and families with older relatives. Takes about 30 to 45 minutes.

You'll need

  • This page, printed
  • A map or globe, if you have one

How to run it

  1. Read out one tradition at a time and find the country on the map.
  2. Ask: "Have you ever been there?" and "Would you like this one here?"
  3. Ask about anyone's family traditions from other countries.
  4. Pick a favourite to try, like lighting a candle for St Lucia.

Make it easier

  • Pictures of each country help.
  • Do two or three countries a day instead of all six.

Traditions

  1. Germany: Advent calendars and Christmas markets
  2. Sweden: St Lucia's Day on 13 December, with candles and singing
  3. The Netherlands: Sinterklaas brings presents on 5 December
  4. Italy: La Befana brings sweets on 6 January
  5. Australia: Christmas Day is in summer, often at the beach
  6. Mexico: Las Posadas processions, and piñatas to break
