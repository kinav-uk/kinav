Make your own Christmas bingo

Draw or write 9 Christmas things on a card, then play bingo with the list below.

For care homes, day centres and families with older relatives. Takes about 30 to 45 minutes.

You'll need

  • Paper with a 3 by 3 grid for each player
  • Pens
  • This list, printed and cut up into calling slips

How to run it

  1. Everyone picks 9 things from the list and writes or draws one in each square.
  2. Pull slips out of a hat and call them out.
  3. Cross them off. First to get a line, then a full card, wins.
  4. Have small prizes ready, like a chocolate coin.

Make it easier

  • Fill in the cards in advance for anyone who'd rather not.
  • Call each one twice, and hold the slip up.

Things to call

  1. Robin
  2. Holly
  3. Cracker
  4. Stocking
  5. Snowman
  6. Christmas pudding
  7. Star
  8. Bell
  9. Candle
  10. Reindeer
  11. Mince pie
  12. Present
  13. Turkey
  14. Angel
  15. Sleigh
  16. Snowflake
