Easy Christmas charades

Mime a Christmas job, and everyone guesses. Lots of laughs and no reading needed.

For care homes, day centres and families with older relatives. Takes about 30 to 45 minutes.

You'll need

  • These mimes, written on slips of paper
  • A bowl

How to run it

  1. Pick a slip and mime it, with no talking.
  2. Everyone guesses.
  3. Staff or family can go first to break the ice.
  4. Swap so everyone who wants a turn gets one.

Make it easier

  • Seated mimes are just as fun.
  • Whisper the mime to anyone who can't read the slip.

Mimes

  1. Wrapping a present
  2. Stirring the Christmas pudding
  3. Pulling a cracker
  4. Building a snowman
  5. Putting the star on the tree
  6. Carving the turkey
  7. Singing a carol
  8. Opening a stocking
  9. Ice skating
  10. Hanging up a stocking
  11. Writing a Christmas card
  12. Eating a mince pie
