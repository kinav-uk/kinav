Day 19 of 24

An old Christmas photos afternoon

Bring out old Christmas photos and cards, and let the stories come.

For care homes, day centres and families with older relatives. Takes about 30 to 45 minutes.

You'll need

  • Old family photos or Christmas cards
  • Or pictures of old Christmases from a library book

How to run it

  1. Ask family to send in a few Christmas photos beforehand.
  2. Look at one photo at a time.
  3. Ask: "Who's this? Where was this taken?"
  4. Write a caption with them, to keep with the photo.

Make it easier

  • Bigger copies are easier to see: a phone or tablet can zoom in.
  • Old Christmas cards work just as well as photos.
