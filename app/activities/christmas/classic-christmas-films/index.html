Day 9 of 24

A classic Christmas film afternoon

Old favourites, a blanket and a mince pie. Then a chat about the films people loved.

For care homes, day centres and families with older relatives. Takes about 30 to 45 minutes.

You'll need

  • A TV or projector, and one of the films below
  • Blankets, tea and mince pies

How to run it

  1. Choose the film together from the list.
  2. Show it in two halves, with a break for tea.
  3. Afterwards ask: "Where did you first see it?"
  4. Ask about going to the pictures when they were young.

Make it easier

  • Turn the subtitles on.
  • A short film like The Snowman (about half an hour) suits a shorter afternoon.

Classic Christmas films

  1. It's a Wonderful Life (1946)
  2. Miracle on 34th Street (1947)
  3. Scrooge, with Alastair Sim (1951)
  4. White Christmas (1954)
  5. The Snowman (1982)
  6. The Muppet Christmas Carol (1992)
