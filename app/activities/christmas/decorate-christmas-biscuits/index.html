Decorate Christmas biscuits

Plain biscuits, icing and sprinkles: quick to set up, and everyone gets to eat their work.

For care homes, day centres and families with older relatives. Takes about 30 to 45 minutes.

You'll need

  • Plain biscuits
  • Ready-made icing in tubes, or icing sugar and water
  • Sprinkles and small sweets
  • Plates and napkins

How to run it

  1. Check everyone's dietary needs first.
  2. Give each person a plate with two or three biscuits.
  3. Ice, decorate and admire.
  4. Eat them with a cup of tea, or wrap them as gifts for visitors.

Make it easier

  • Icing tubes are easier to hold than knives.
  • Pre-iced biscuits just need sprinkles on top.
