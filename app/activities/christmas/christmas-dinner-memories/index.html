What was on your Christmas table?

A conversation about Christmas dinners past: who cooked, what was served, and who got the wishbone.

For care homes, day centres and families with older relatives. Takes about 30 to 45 minutes.

You'll need

  • A cup of tea and a comfortable spot
  • Optional: a few pictures of Christmas food to pass round

How to run it

  1. Sit in a small group or one to one.
  2. Ask one question at a time and give plenty of time to answer.
  3. Share your own memories too, so it feels like a chat.
  4. Note any lovely stories to share with family.

Make it easier

  • Pictures help people who find words harder.
  • There are no wrong answers: every Christmas was different.

Questions to ask

  1. Who did the cooking on Christmas Day?
  2. Did you have turkey, goose, chicken or something else?
  3. What did you have for pudding?
  4. Was there a sixpence in the Christmas pudding?
  5. Who sat where around the table?
  6. What did you drink with Christmas dinner?
  7. Who got the wishbone?
  8. What did you eat for tea on Boxing Day?
