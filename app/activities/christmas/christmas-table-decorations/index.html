Make a Christmas table decoration

Pine cones, ribbon and greenery: a simple centrepiece for the Christmas table.

For care homes, day centres and families with older relatives. Takes about 30 to 45 minutes.

You'll need

  • Pine cones
  • Ribbon, and sprigs of greenery
  • A small dish or jar for each person
  • Battery tea lights (not real candles)

How to run it

  1. Collect pine cones and greenery together, or bring them in.
  2. Arrange them in a dish with a battery tea light.
  3. Tie a ribbon round the dish.
  4. Use them on the tables for Christmas dinner.

Make it easier

  • Lay everything out on a tray so it's in easy reach.
  • People can choose and pass, and someone else can arrange.
