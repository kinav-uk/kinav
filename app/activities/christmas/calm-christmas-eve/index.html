A calm Christmas Eve

A gentle evening after a busy month: soft music, a story and a warm drink.

For care homes, day centres and families with older relatives. Takes about 30 to 45 minutes.

You'll need

  • Soft Christmas music
  • Battery candles or fairy lights
  • Hot chocolate or tea
  • A blanket

How to run it

  1. Dim the main lights and switch on the fairy lights.
  2. Play quiet carols.
  3. Read a short story, or share a Christmas Eve memory.
  4. Say goodnight with "Happy Christmas".

Make it easier

  • Keep the room calm and not too busy.
  • Some people prefer to spend it one to one.
