Care home activity ideas for January

A fresh start: cosy afternoons, Burns Night and planning the year ahead. Easy ideas for care homes, day centres and families with older relatives.

Dates to plan around in January 2027

  • 1 January: New Year's Day
  • 25 January: Burns Night

8 activity ideas

  1. New Year memories
    How did you see the New Year in? Talk about first-footing, Auld Lang Syne and resolutions.
  2. A Burns Night afternoon
    Shortbread, a poem read aloud, Scottish songs and a tartan to pass round.
  3. Winter warmers taste test
    Try three soups or hot drinks and vote for a favourite.
  4. The year ahead calendar
    Make a big wall calendar together and fill in birthdays and outings.
  5. Snow memories
    Sledging, snowmen and the worst winters people remember.
  6. Indoor bowls
    Soft balls and a target on the floor. Play seated or standing.
  7. Seed catalogue browsing
    Look through seed catalogues and choose what to grow this spring.
  8. Old toys and games
    Bring out marbles, jacks, cards and dominoes, and talk about childhood games.

Check dietary needs before anything is eaten, and adapt each idea so everyone can join in.
