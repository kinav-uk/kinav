Paying for care in County Durham

In England, the council may help pay for care if the person has less than £23,250 in savings and other assets. Below £14,250, these don't count at all. The value of their home is usually ignored for the first 12 weeks in a care home, and always if a partner or certain relatives still live there.

For free help with the financial assessment or a council charging decision, contact Citizens Advice (CAB) on 0800 144 8848.

Day centres and respite in County Durham

Day centres offer company, activities and a meal, often with transport, and give family carers a regular break. Many, especially those run or funded by the council, only take people referred by a social worker, so ask at the needs assessment. Others let you book and pay privately. Ask about respite care too: short stays so carers can rest.

Day centres aren't usually rated by CQC, so they aren't listed on Kinav. Ask County Durham adult social care, your social worker, or local charities such as Age UK and Alzheimer's Society what's available nearby.
