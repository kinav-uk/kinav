Ask Darlington Borough Council for a care needs assessment

Anyone in Darlington who seems to need care or support can ask Darlington Borough Council for an assessment. It's free, whatever their savings, and it's how the council decides what help it can arrange.

Ask online on Darlington Borough Council's website, or let Kinav write the letter for you, ready to email.

Go to Darlington Borough Council Write the letter

How it works

  1. Ask adult social care
    Contact Darlington Borough Council online, by phone or with a letter. You can ask on their behalf.
  2. Prepare
    Keep a diary for a week of what they need help with, day and night, and write down what family do now and can't keep doing.
  3. The assessment
    At home, by phone or by video. Family can be there. If they'd struggle to take part and have no one to help, the council must offer an advocate.
  4. The decision
    The council tells you in writing whether they meet the national eligibility criteria. If they do, it writes a care and support plan with you.
  5. Paying for care
    A separate financial assessment works out what they pay. With savings over £23,250 they usually pay the full cost at first; below £14,250, savings aren't counted.
  6. If you disagree
    Ask for a review, then complain if needed. After that, the Local Government and Social Care Ombudsman can look into it, free.
