Paying for care in Wigan

In England, the council may help pay for care if the person has less than £23,250 in savings and other assets. Below £14,250, these don't count at all. The value of their home is usually ignored for the first 12 weeks in a care home, and always if a partner or certain relatives still live there.

For free help with the financial assessment or a council charging decision, contact Citizens Advice (CAB) on 0800 144 8848.
