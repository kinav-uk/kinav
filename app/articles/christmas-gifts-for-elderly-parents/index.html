Christmas gifts for elderly parents and grandparents (UK)

Stuck for a present for Mum, Dad, Nan or Grandad, especially one who "doesn't need anything"? Here are thoughtful gift ideas that bring people together, bring back memories and make everyday life a little easier.

Tips for choosing

  • Think about what they enjoy now, not just what they used to do.
  • For someone in a care home, ask the staff what's allowed and what space there is.
  • Label photos and gifts clearly, with big writing.
  • If they really don't want things, give your time: it's usually what they want most.
