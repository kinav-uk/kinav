Help with heating costs for older people this winter (2026 to 2027)

Several payments help older people with heating bills over winter, and most are paid automatically. But some depend on getting Pension Credit, which many people never claim. Here's what's available in England and Wales this winter, and how to check your parent gets it.

Winter Fuel Payment

Your parent can get a Winter Fuel Payment if they were born on or before 27 June 1960 and live in England or Wales. Most people get it automatically. A letter says how much, and most are paid in November or December 2026.

  • £300 if they were born before 28 September 1946, and £200 if born between 28 September 1946 and 27 June 1960.
  • They may get less if they live with someone else who also qualifies, unless they get a benefit such as Pension Credit.
  • In a care home, it's £150 or £100, depending on age.
  • If their total income is over £35,000, HMRC takes it back through their tax. A partner's income doesn't count.

Cold Weather Payment

£25 for each 7-day period when the average temperature where they live is, or is forecast to be, 0°C or below. It runs from 1 November 2026 to 31 March 2027.

It's paid automatically to people who get certain benefits, including Pension Credit. You can check whether a payment is due for their postcode on GOV.UK.

Warm Home Discount

£150 off the electricity bill. Your parent may be eligible if, on 23 August 2026, they got Pension Credit, Universal Credit, Housing Benefit or income-related Employment and Support Allowance, and their name is on the electricity bill.

  • Letters go out between late October 2026 and early January 2027.
  • If the letter says they don't need to do anything, the supplier adds the discount by 31 March 2027.
  • If the letter asks them to call the helpline, they must do it by 26 February 2027.
  • No letter by early January? Contact the Warm Home Discount Scheme.

Pension Credit: the one that unlocks the rest

Pension Credit tops up a low income. Getting it can also mean Cold Weather Payments, the Warm Home Discount, help with council tax and housing costs, and a free TV licence for over-75s.

Many older people who could get it never claim. Use Kinav's free Benefits calculator to check. They can claim online or by phone, and a claim can be backdated up to 3 months.

Other free help

  • The Priority Services Register: free extra help from energy and water companies, such as warning of planned power cuts. Ask their supplier to add them.
  • Free advice on saving energy and grants for insulation or heating: call the free government energy advice line on 0800 444 202.
  • Age UK Advice Line: 0800 678 1602, free, every day.
  • Citizens Advice: 0800 144 8848 (England), for help with bills and benefits.

Scotland and Northern Ireland

Scotland has its own payments, such as the Pension Age Winter Heating Payment and the Winter Heating Payment, instead of the Winter Fuel and Cold Weather Payments. Northern Ireland has different schemes too. Check mygov.scot or nidirect.gov.uk.
